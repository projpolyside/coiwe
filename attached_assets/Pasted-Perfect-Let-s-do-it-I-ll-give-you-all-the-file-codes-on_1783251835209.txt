          <p className="text-gray-700 mb-6">Market capitalization is one of the most important metrics when evaluating cryptocurrencies, yet many new investors overlook its significance.</p>
          
          <h2 className="text-2xl font-semibold text-gray-900 mt-8 mb-4">What is Market Cap?</h2>
          <p className="text-gray-700 mb-6">Market capitalization is calculated by multiplying the current price by the circulating supply. It represents the total market value of all coins in circulation.</p>
          
          <h2 className="text-2xl font-semibold text-gray-900 mt-8 mb-4">Market Cap Categories</h2>
          <ul className="list-disc pl-6 space-y-2 text-gray-700 mb-6">
            <li><strong>Large Cap (&gt;$10B):</strong> Generally more stable, established projects with proven track records</li>
            <li><strong>Mid Cap ($1B-$10B):</strong> Balance of growth potential and risk, more volatile than large cap</li>
            <li><strong>Small Cap (&lt;$1B):</strong> Higher risk, higher reward potential, prone to volatility and speculation</li>
          </ul>
          
          <h2 className="text-2xl font-semibold text-gray-900 mt-8 mb-4">Fully Diluted Valuation (FDV)</h2>
          <p className="text-gray-700 mb-6">FDV shows what the market cap would be if all tokens were released. A large gap between market cap and FDV signals future selling pressure and potential price impact.</p>
          
          <h2 className="text-2xl font-semibold text-gray-900 mt-8 mb-4">Why It Matters for Investors</h2>
          <p className="text-gray-700 mb-6">A smaller market cap project needs less capital inflow to achieve significant price appreciation. However, they also tend to be more volatile and prone to manipulation. Understanding this helps you size positions appropriately.</p>
          
          <p className="text-gray-700">Always compare market cap in context with other metrics like trading volume and utility to get the complete picture of a cryptocurrency's potential.</p>
        </article>
      </div>
    </div>
  );
}
